HOW TO PLAY

Goal: tunnel through the dirt, collect the Beads quota shown in the top bar, then reach the BAY exit before TIME runs out.

  • Move & dig: arrows / WASD / the d-pad. Walk into brown dirt to dig a path. The view scrolls to follow Bot 106.
  • Beads are the amber gems — walk onto one to grab it. Collect the number in BEADS x/total.
  • When you hit the quota the screen flashes white and the hidden BAY opens (bright green) — step into it to clear the cave.
  • Drums (barrels) fall and roll like boulders. Dig the dirt under one and step aside — being under a falling drum is fatal.
  • Push a resting drum sideways into an empty gap (just walk into it). Space / DIG + a direction digs or grabs without moving.
  • Avoid Sparks (red) and Tickflies (green) — one touch kills. Drop a drum on a Tickfly to burst it into Beads.
